The Free 22-0 AFL Team Generator & Roster Builder

Can You Go 22-0? is a free browser game built around a simple dare: use the draft wheel — a random AFL team generator that deals you a club and an era on every spin — to build an all-time roster of legends, then simulate a full 22-game home-and-away season. Win them all and you've done what no real AFL club ever has: a perfect 22-0.

How to play

  1. Spin the wheel. The generator lands on a club + era combo and shows you that squad's players.
  2. Draft one player from the board and place him in your lineup. Skips are limited, so spend them wisely.
  3. Fill all six positions — Key Defender, Half-Back, Midfielder, Ruck, Key Forward, Small Forward — balancing star power with team chemistry.
  4. Pick your coach. Each coach boosts a different style of roster.
  5. Simulate the 22-game season and see how far your record carries — then take an unbeaten team into the Finals.

Frequently asked questions

What is Can You Go 22-0?

It's a free AFL roster builder and season simulator. You draft an all-time team of legends from every era using a randomized draft wheel, then simulate a 22-game season. The goal — and the name — is finishing a perfect 22-0.

How does the 22-0 AFL team generator work?

Every spin randomly generates a club and a decade, and you draft one player from that combination. Repeat until all six starting spots are filled. No two runs deal the same boards, so every roster is unique.

What's the best team to go 22-0?

Raw star power isn't enough — team chemistry matters. Rosters that spread across eras, pair a dominant ruck with sharpshooters and a true ball magnet, and match the coach's style win more games. A six-legend lineup with clashing play styles will drop games a balanced team wouldn't.

Is Can You Go 22-0 free?

Yes — completely free, no download, no sign-up. It runs in your browser on any device.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game is fully responsive and plays the same on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
